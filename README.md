# Simple Interest Calculator

## Overview
The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple application designed to calculate the interest earned or payable on a principal amount over a specific period at a given rate of interest.

It uses the simple interest formula to calculate the interest and the total amount.

## Formula

The formula for calculating Simple Interest is:

**SI = (P × R × T) / 100**

Where:
- **P** = Principal Amount
- **R** = Rate of Interest (per annum)
- **T** = Time Period (in years)
- **SI** = Simple Interest

The total amount is calculated as:

**Total Amount = Principal Amount + Simple Interest**

## Features
- Accepts the principal amount.
- Accepts the annual rate of interest.
- Accepts the time period in years.
- Calculates simple interest accurately.
- Displays the total amount (principal + interest).
- Simple and user-friendly interface.

## Example Calculation

**Input:**
- Principal Amount = ₹10,000
- Rate of Interest = 5%
- Time Period = 2 years

**Calculation:**

SI = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100

SI = ₹1,000

**Output:**
- Simple Interest = ₹1,000
- Total Amount = ₹11,000

## How to Use
1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual rate of interest.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Click the Calculate button (if applicable).
5. View the calculated simple interest and total amount.

## Conclusion
The Simple Interest Calculator provides an easy and efficient way to calculate simple interest. It helps users understand the basic concept of simple interest and perform financial calculations quickly and accurately.
